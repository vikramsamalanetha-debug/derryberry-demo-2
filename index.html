Derryberry Auction Co. is a family-run auction house based on Highway 100 West in Decaturville. We handle vehicle sales, full estate liquidations, and farm equipment for sellers throughout Decatur, Henderson, and the surrounding counties.

Every lot gets looked at, described honestly, and sold to the person willing to pay for it — no reserves hiding behind fine print, no surprises at pickup.

  • — Vehicle titles and paperwork handled on-site
  • — Full-service estate and downsizing sales
  • — Serving Decatur County and West Tennessee
